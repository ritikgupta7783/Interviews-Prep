## 80/20 rule

Prepare the P0 questions first. For each project, be able to: (1) give the pitch, (2) draw the architecture, (3) trace one key workflow through code, (4) explain one failure/security issue, and (5) name one justified improvement. Do not spend early prep time on irrelevant microservice/Kubernetes/distributed-system hypotheticals for projects that do not use them.
